WoD20 — V20 Vampire NPC Builder v0.1.7 — Easier to find in Manage Modules

The module now appears as “WoD20 — V20 Vampire NPC Builder” in Foundry's module list. Only the display title and release/documentation files changed; NPC creation, saved Actors, optional portraits, Tokenizer 2 behavior and the module ID are unchanged.

Before updating
- Back up your Foundry world, installed module and uploaded/generated artwork.
- Update through the existing manifest, enable it in the intended world, restart/reload the world and hard-refresh your browser. Module settings and Actor data are not migrated.

Known limitations
- v0.1.7 module-manager presentation and full WoD20 Actor/Discipline round trips remain unverified in a live Foundry v14 world. Automated and mocked tests are not equivalent to live validation.
- Tokenizer 2 is optional and has not been fully verified live with this release.
- The module is unofficial, experimental and supplied as-is; see the included license and third-party notices.
